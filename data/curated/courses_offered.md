# Courses Offered at DITM (all programmes, branches and courses list)

Sources: DITM website courses page (https://www.ditmcollege.org/courses.html), AICTE Extension of Approval 2025-26, DITM fee structure and DITM syllabus 2026-27.

DITM offers the following courses / programmes (complete list):
- B.Tech (4 years): CSE, AI & Machine Learning, AI & Data Science, ECE, Electrical (EE), Civil, Mechanical (ME)
- MBA (2 years)
- BBA
- BCA
- Diploma / Polytechnic (3 years): Computer Science, Electrical, Civil, Mechanical
- D.Pharma (Diploma in Pharmacy, 2 years)

Details of each course (seats, fees, affiliation) are below.

## B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) - 4 years, 8 semesters, affiliated to MDU Rohtak
1. B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) - 180 seats
2. B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML) - 120 seats
3. B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS) - 60 seats
4. B.Tech Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) - 60 seats
5. B.Tech Electrical Engineering (EE) - 60 seats
6. B.Tech Civil Engineering (CE) - 60 seats
7. B.Tech Mechanical Engineering (ME) - 60 seats
B.Tech fee: Rs. 78,000 per year (same for all branches).

## Management and Computer Applications - affiliated to MDU Rohtak
8. MBA (Master of Business Administration) - 2 years, 60 seats, Rs. 76,000 per year
9. BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) - 180 seats, Rs. 56,000 per year
10. BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) - 180 seats, Rs. 61,000 per year
BBA and BCA syllabus is given for 3 years (Sem 1-6); the fee sheet lists fees for 4 years (NEP 4-year option). Confirm the duration with admissions.

## Diploma (Polytechnic) - 3 years, affiliated to HSBTE Panchkula
11. Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering - 30 seats
12. Diploma in Electrical Engineering - 30 seats
13. Diploma in Civil Engineering - 30 seats
14. Diploma in Mechanical Engineering - 30 seats
Diploma fee: Rs. 31,000 per year.

## Pharmacy
15. D.Pharma (Diploma in Pharmacy) - 2 years, Rs. 76,000 per year (listed in the DITM fee structure; confirm availability with admissions).

How to apply: online at https://www.ditmcollege.org/admissionform.html or call 9318389921.
