# DITM Fee Structure (per year)

Source: https://www.ditmcollege.org/documents/fees.pdf (scanned document linked as "Fees Structure" on the Courses page; transcribed manually). The document does not state the academic session. Fees should be confirmed with the DITM admissions/accounts office before payment. Hostel and transport charges are not included in this document.

## B.Tech (CSE / AI-ML / AI-DS / Civil / ME / ECE / EE) fee
The B.Tech fee is the same for all branches: CSE, AI & ML, AI & Data Science, Civil, Mechanical (ME), ECE and Electrical (EE).
- Tuition fee: Rs. 58,000 per year
- Development fund: Rs. 16,000 per year
- Miscellaneous: Rs. 4,000 per year
- Total B.Tech fee: Rs. 78,000 per year (1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th year). Approx. Rs. 3,12,000 for the full 4-year course.

## Diploma (ME / Civil / CSE / EE) fee
- Tuition fee: Rs. 18,700 per year
- Development fund: Rs. 5,600 per year
- Miscellaneous: Rs. 6,700 per year
- Total Diploma fee: Rs. 31,000 per year (1st, 2nd and 3rd year).

## BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) fee
- Tuition fee: Rs. 37,000 per year
- Development fund: Rs. 8,000 per year
- Miscellaneous: Rs. 11,000 per year
- Total BBA fee: Rs. 56,000 per year (fee listed for 1st to 4th year).

## BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) fee
- Tuition fee: Rs. 42,000 per year
- Development fund: Rs. 8,000 per year
- Miscellaneous: Rs. 11,000 per year
- Total BCA fee: Rs. 61,000 per year (fee listed for 1st to 4th year).

## MBA (Master of Business Administration) fee
- Tuition fee: Rs. 58,000 per year
- Development fund: Rs. 14,000 per year
- Miscellaneous: Rs. 4,000 per year
- Total MBA fee: Rs. 76,000 per year (1st and 2nd year).

## D.Pharma (Diploma in Pharmacy) fee
- Tuition fee: Rs. 60,000 per year
- Development fund: Rs. 12,000 per year
- Miscellaneous: Rs. 4,000 per year
- Total D.Pharma fee: Rs. 76,000 per year (1st and 2nd year).
