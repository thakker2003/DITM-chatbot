Delhi Institute of Technology and Management Message Home Messages Chairman's Message It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to the Delhi Institute of Technology & Management (DITM) — a premier institution dedicated to excellence in technical and professional education. At DITM, we believe that education is not merely about earning a degree, but about transforming individuals into competent professionals and responsible citizens. We aim to cultivate a dynamic environment where learning, innovation, and values thrive together. We offer a wide array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs including B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML), Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS), Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical Engineering (EE), and Civil Engineering, along with professional programs like BBA, MBA, BCA, and MCA. Each program is designed to keep pace with industry demands and global standards. Located in the education-rich hub of Delhi-NCR and affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), our institution is driven by the mission to provide value-based education backed by a forward-thinking approach and world-class infrastructure. As the Chairman, I take pride in the culture of dedication, discipline, and integrity that defines DITM. Our faculty, mentors, and support systems work tirelessly to ensure that students not only gain technical knowledge but also develop the skills, confidence, and ethics necessary for lifelong success. I invite you to join the DITM community, where ambition is nurtured, talent is guided, and futures are built with purpose and passion. – Sh. S.K. Jain Chairman, DITM Director General's Message At DITM, we believe that education must go hand-in-hand with employability, innovation, and integrity. As Director, it is my privilege to lead an institution that is deeply committed to nurturing talent, igniting curiosity, and preparing students for a fast-changing professional world. We offer a comprehensive portfolio of programs — including B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Electronics & Communication (ECE), Electrical Engineering (EE), Civil Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML), Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS), as well as BBA, MBA, BCA, and MCA. These programs are designed not just to impart knowledge, but to develop the skills, adaptability, and mindset needed to thrive in the real world. One of our key focus areas is training and placement. At DITM, we ensure our students receive rigorous training, soft skills grooming, industry exposure, and placement assistance from day one. Our Training & Placement Cell works proactively with top recruiters, conducts mock interviews, resume-building sessions, industrial visits, and internships to make every student job-ready and confident. With a strong academic framework, modern infrastructure, and a dedicated faculty team, we foster an environment of excellence, discipline, and innovation. We emphasize not just what students learn, but how they apply it — because success today demands more than just theoretical understanding. I invite aspiring students to join DITM, Delhi-NCR, and take the first step toward a future filled with opportunities, transformation, and impact. – Dr. Rajesh Garg Director General, DITM PhD CSE, M.Tech(CSE) (GJUS&T, Hisar), B.Tech(CSE) (NIT, Kurushetra), GATE Published More than 28 research paper, National & Internationl Journals and Conferences.
