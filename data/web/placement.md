# Placement Cell

Source: https://www.ditmcollege.org/placement.html

Delhi Institute of Technology and Management Placement Cell Home Facilities Placement Cell Placement Cell DITM, Sonepat has made significant strides in improving its placement ecosystem, aiming to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements. The college has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that actively collaborates with top recruiters to organize campus drives, internship opportunities, and skill development workshops. Over the years, more than 70 companies have participated in recruitment events, including renowned names like TCS, Wipro, Infosys, HCL Technologies, Bosch, Tech Mahindra, Genpact, Tata Motors, IBM, NIIT Technologies, and Sony. The highest package offered has reportedly reached ₹12.5 LPA for B.Tech students, while the average package remains in the range of ₹4 to ₹6 LPA. Some students with outstanding technical and communication skills have even secured international opportunities with packages soaring up to ₹25 LPA. The placement cell also ensures students are groomed through mock interviews, aptitude sessions, and resume-building guidance, enhancing their employability. Despite these strengths, student feedback presents a mixed picture. While many appreciate the exposure to reputed companies and supportive faculty, others have expressed concerns about uneven placement assistance across departments or promotional discrepancies. Nevertheless, students from branches like Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Electronics Engineering have consistently shown strong placement performance, particularly those who proactively engage in coding, project work, internships, and co-curricular competitions. In addition, the institution promotes entrepreneurship and innovation through various clubs and technical societies, empowering students to pursue start-ups and research-based careers. Overall, placements at DITM are best leveraged by students who take initiative, polish their technical and interpersonal skills, and take full advantage of the institute’s resources.
